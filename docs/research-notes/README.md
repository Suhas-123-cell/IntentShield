# Research notes

Historical drafts and demo material. They are kept for the record. They are not the project's current claims.

- [paper-draft.md](paper-draft.md): the first complete-mediation paper draft. Its own status note says the claim is not supported and its MSB numbers are superseded.
- [artifact-paper.md](artifact-paper.md): a skeleton of an artifact or tool paper, started 2026-10-14. Numbers in it may be out of date.
- [icse27-demo/](icse27-demo/): LaTeX source, references and a video script for a demo-track paper. It is a draft.
- [contribution-history.md](contribution-history.md): the earlier, long version of `docs/contribution.md`, moved here whole on 2026-11-11: every dated verdict, the tables later superseded (marked SUPERSEDED) and the day-by-day account of the hook study. Section numbers such as §5, §7, §9.5, §12.1 and §13 in other documents refer to this file.
- [hook-fidelity/](hook-fidelity/hook-fidelity-study.md): the working notes of the hook study (the full study, the guard corpus, prevalence, the stub endpoint, the coverage re-run and the verdict-translation notes). They are written in the study's own vocabulary (rounds, lanes, critics) and are the record behind the [short report](../hook-fidelity-report.md), which is the document to read.

**Paths under `artifacts/`.** Several notes here cite files under `artifacts/` (raw run logs, dataset checkouts, trained models). That directory is kept by the author, not in the repository. Where a note gives a number from such a file, the number cannot be re-checked from the repository.

For what the project claims now, read [../contribution.md](../contribution.md) (the verdict), [../benchmarks.md](../benchmarks.md) (the numbers and limits) and [../hook-fidelity-report.md](../hook-fidelity-report.md) (the hook study).
