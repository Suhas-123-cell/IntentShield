False-allow = a harmful call the guard allowed (or errored on, which every harness here runs). False-deny/false-ask = a benign call the guard denied/asked about. **NOT-RUN** = the guard was never invoked on that row; those rows are excluded from every rate. Wilson 95% intervals in brackets, computed unrounded from k/n at display. The ask/deny split is printed next to every false-allow: an ask is not a stop on a build whose harness runs, drops or hangs on it (see the effective-protection table at the end).
